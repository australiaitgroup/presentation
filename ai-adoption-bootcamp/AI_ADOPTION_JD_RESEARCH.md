# INTERVIEW QUESTION THEMES (From Research)

## Business Judgment
- How would you determine which AI tools are appropriate for a specific team/workflow?
- Describe a time you decided NOT to implement an AI solution. Why?
- How do you balance AI enthusiasm with practical business needs?

## Measurable Impact
- Share a specific example where AI measurably improved your work
- How did the process work before AI? What changed? What improved?
- How do you measure whether AI adoption is actually producing results?

## Data Privacy & Trust
- How do you handle sensitive company data when using AI tools?
- What governance frameworks have you implemented for AI usage?
- How do you ensure responsible AI usage across an organization?

## Change Management
- How do you handle resistance to AI adoption?
- Describe your approach to stakeholder engagement during an AI rollout
- How do you sustain adoption after initial implementation?

## Training & Coaching
- How do you design training for audiences with different AI maturity levels?
- What's your approach to 1:1 AI coaching?
- How do you create effective prompt libraries for different roles?

## Strategic Vision
- How would you build an AI adoption roadmap for a 500-person company?
- How do you prioritize which business processes to apply AI to first?
- What metrics would you use to track organizational AI maturity?

# KEY TAKEAWAY: What Employers ACTUALLY Want

**The #1 pattern across all JDs:** This is NOT a technical AI engineering role. Employers want someone who can:

1. **TEACH** — Design and deliver training programs at scale
2. **COACH** — Work 1:1 with employees on real workflows
3. **MEASURE** — Track adoption metrics and prove ROI
4. **COMMUNICATE** — Translate AI capabilities for executives and frontline workers
5. **MANAGE CHANGE** — Handle resistance, build champions, sustain adoption
6. **USE AI TOOLS** — Be proficient with ChatGPT, Copilot, etc. (not build models)
7. **CREATE RESOURCES** — Build prompt libraries, user guides, FAQs, training materials

**The role sits at the intersection of:** Training/L&D + Change Management + AI Literacy + Business Strategy

**It does NOT typically require:** ML engineering, model training, deep coding skills, or PhD-level AI research.
